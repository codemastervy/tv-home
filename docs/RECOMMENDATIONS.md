# Where recommendations come from, and what is actually readable

## Android TvContract (no key, no account)
Apps publish to the TV provider: **Watch Next** (what you are part way through) and **preview channels**
(rows an app asks the home screen to show). A home app with the `READ_TV_LISTINGS` permission can read both.
The home reads them per app package: the "What's on X" row starts with what X itself published (personalised by X),
Continue watching comes from Watch Next.

Checked on the API 34 Android TV emulator with this app installed as a normal, non-system app:
- `READ_TV_LISTINGS` (a normal runtime grant, asked once) was **enough** for Watch Next, preview channels
  (`content://android.media.tv/channel`) and preview programs (`.../preview_program`) of other apps.
  `READ_EPG_DATA` was not needed and is not requested. Without the grant the provider returns nothing; the app reports that.
- The emulator image's own YouTube app had published 3 channels (a recommended-for-you channel, a trending channel and a music channel)
  with 15 programs, all read with title, description, artwork and genre.
  `tests/emulator/published_feed.py` also seeds channels as the shell and checks they are read back.
- Per-program fields used: title, short description, poster / thumbnail art, canonical genre, the app's own `intent_uri`
  (opened only in the app that owns the program; if absent or refused the app is launched instead).
- Not proven: what Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video and Apple TV publish on a real TV. Apps decide this; many publish only
  Watch Next, some nothing. A platform with nothing published shows no row unless TMDB is on.
- Google's own home banner and "For you" rows are generated by Google's home app and are not exposed, so they cannot be reused.

## TMDB (optional, off by default)
With a user-supplied free API key (`tmdb.api_key` in the git-ignored `config.json`, or typed in Settings on the TV)
the home calls `api.themoviedb.org` only. `discover` is filtered by `with_watch_providers` for your platform and
`watch_region` (default `NZ`), once for included-with-subscription and once for rent / buy, so rent and buy titles
are labelled. Rows are ranked by the genres of My List and Continue watching. Search looks titles up and keeps only
those available on your platforms in your region. TMDB gives no deep links, so a result launches the app.

*This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Streaming availability data is provided by JustWatch via TMDB.*

## Gemini
The Gemini button and "Ask Gemini" in search start the TV's own assistant through Android's assist intents
(`ACTION_ASSIST`, with the typed words as an intent extra only). Gemini is a system feature; this app contains no AI
and calls no AI service. If the TV has no assistant the button says so.
